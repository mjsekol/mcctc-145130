# Electronics Bench

Invented sample document for this course.

## The bench equipment

The soldering station, the bench power supply, the digital oscilloscope, and the servo
and sensor bin live at the electronics bench. The soldering station and the oscilloscope
carry a training hold.

## Loan length

Electronics bench equipment is a fourteen day loan, which is the longest loan in the
catalog. Project work in electronics runs across weeks rather than days.

## How many at once

You may sign out up to three items from the electronics bench at one time, because a
power supply, a scope, and a parts bin are one working setup rather than three separate
projects.

## Soldering

Solder in the fume extractor's airflow. The extractor is the box with the grille, and it
helps only when the work is in front of it rather than beside it. Wash your hands after
soldering.

A cold joint looks dull and grainy. A good joint looks shiny and cone shaped. If you are
not sure, reflow it. Reflowing costs ten seconds and a bad joint costs an afternoon of
debugging.

## The power supply

Set the current limit before you connect anything. The current limit is the setting that
turns a wiring mistake into a supply that refuses to deliver current, rather than a
component that releases smoke.

## The oscilloscope

Probe ground clips connect to circuit ground, not to whatever is convenient. Clipping a
scope ground to a live point is the fastest way to damage the scope, the circuit, or
both.

## The parts bin

Take what you need and write what you took on the bin card. The bin is restocked from
the card. An empty bin with a blank card gets restocked with nothing.
