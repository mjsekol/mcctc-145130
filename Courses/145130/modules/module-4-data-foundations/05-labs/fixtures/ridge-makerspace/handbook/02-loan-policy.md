# Ridge Creek Makerspace: Loan Policy

Invented sample document for this course.

## What may be borrowed

Every item in the catalog may be borrowed unless it is marked out of service. The
catalog is the list the kiosk shows, and it is the only list that counts. A sticker on a
shelf is not a catalog entry.

## How long

Loan length is set per item and printed on the item tag. Fabrication equipment is a
seven day loan. The resin printer is a five day loan because it needs the ventilation
cabinet. Electronics bench equipment is a fourteen day loan. Media kits are a three day
loan. Laptop carts are a two day loan.

## How many at once

A member may hold two items at one time. If you need a third item, ask a staff member,
who may approve it and record the reason in the kiosk.

## Returns

Return the item to the staff member on duty, not to the shelf. The staff member records
the return in the kiosk. A return that is not recorded is not a return, and the item
stays on your record as still out.

## Late items

An item is late when the current date is past the due date and the item has not been
recorded as returned. A late item does not carry a fine. It carries a hold: you may not
borrow anything else until the late item comes back.

A member with two late returns in one grading period meets with a staff member before
borrowing again.

## Renewals

You may renew once if nobody is waiting for the item. A renewal creates a new loan with
a new due date. It does not extend the old loan, because the old loan is the record of
what actually happened.

## Batteries

Camera kits, microphone kits, and laptop carts go out charged and come back charged. If
you cannot charge an item before returning it, say so at the kiosk so the next member is
not surprised.
