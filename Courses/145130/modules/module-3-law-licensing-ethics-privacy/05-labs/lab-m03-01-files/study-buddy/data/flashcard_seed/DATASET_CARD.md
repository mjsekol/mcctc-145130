**INVENTED DATASET. Written for the MCCTC 145130 Module 3 licensing fixture.

published every day, and the gap this one has is the gap they usually have.

## What the Study Buddy team used it for

Testing the parser. The team loaded the chemistry rows, ran them through
`make_cards.py`, and checked that the cards came out right. No part of this
dataset ships in the finished product.

## Where the items came from

| Portion | Source | Terms on that source |
|---|---|---|
| About 55% | Written by the collectors | Contributed under the dataset license |
| About 30% | Public school course outlines posted by three districts | No license stated on the pages |
| About 15% | A publisher's free online glossary | The site's terms page says the glossary is for personal study use and may not be redistributed |

## Reading note for auditors

The license on a dataset covers what the collectors had the right to license. It
does not change the terms attached to material they gathered from somewhere else.
Two questions to answer before this dataset is used for anything that ships:

1. What does the dataset license require of someone who publishes a changed
   version of the dataset?
2. Does the fact that the team only used the dataset for testing, and shipped
   none of it, change the answer? Write down which facts your answer depends on.
