## 7. Writing for somebody who is annoyed

**The federal plain language guidelines** · `https://www.plainlanguage.gov/guidelines/`
· **[VERIFY]**. Free, no account.

**Why this one, in a research week.** Your task wording is read by somebody who is
slightly nervous and does not want to look foolish. The three rules from Tuesday's
lesson are in here in more detail: address the reader directly, say what to do
rather than what happened, and use words the reader already has.

**The test to run on your four tasks.** Read each one out loud to your partner with
no preamble. **Any task you have to explain before reading is too long.**

**Time.** 25 minutes. **Level.** Remediation.

## 9. The official documentation page for the week

`https://docs.python.org/3/library/statistics.html` · **Confident.**

**Why a statistics page in a week with no code in it.** Because of one thing you
should not do: compute a mean of five numbers and report it as though it described
a population.

**Read `mean` and `median` and stop.** Then answer this: with times on task of 41,
55, 62, 70 and 220 seconds, which of the two describes the group better, and what
does the answer tell you about that fifth session?

**The answer matters more than the functions.** The median is 62 and the mean is
89.6, and the gap between them is one participant who nearly gave up. **A single
number hid the finding.**

**Time.** 15 minutes. **Level.** Extension.

## 10. Who your five participants are not

**W3C Web Accessibility Initiative, introduction to accessibility** ·
`https://www.w3.org/WAI/fundamentals/accessibility-intro/` · **[VERIFY]**.

**Why this one, this week.** Thursday's note says five people cannot find a problem
that affects a small group, and that this includes most accessibility problems.
This is the shortest honest introduction to what those problems are.

**What to take from it, and it is one sentence for your report:** your five
participants almost certainly all used a mouse, a screen, and English, and every
finding you have is a finding about people who do.

**Time.** 30 minutes. **Level.** Extension.
