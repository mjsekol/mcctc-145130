## The situation

A team ran their eight acceptance cases. They wrote the results down honestly,
every verdict, including the ones they did not like. Their run record is in the
folder and it is accurate.

Then they gave the record to an AI assistant and asked it to write the acceptance
report for the stakeholder.

**The report is the document that goes out.** The record stays in a folder.

---

## Your job

**Score the report on the five AI Output Evaluation parameters.**

```
Validity        is what it says true, given the record
Relevance       is it about the thing that was asked
Authenticity    is it what it claims to be, from where it claims
Potential Bias  does it treat people or evidence unevenly
Hallucinations  does it contain things that are not there
```

**There is at least one real problem under every one of the five.** There is also
**one item that is genuinely arguable**, and it is not one of the five.

---

## What is in the folder

| File | Read it |
|---|---|
| `run-record.md` | **First.** Eight cases, captured output, the team's own verdicts |
| `generated-acceptance-report.md` | The document you are scoring. Sections are numbered |

**Everything in the folder is invented**, including the team and the stakeholder.
**The record is internally consistent**, which means every claim in the report can
be settled by reading it.

---

## What to hand in

### Five findings, one per parameter

```
Parameter        which of the five
The sentence     quoted, with its section number
The evidence     the case ID and the line in the record that settles it
What it costs    what somebody would do wrong because of it
```

### Plus one entry: the thing you are unsure about

```
The sentence            quoted
The case that it is fine
The case that it is not
What would settle it     the one thing you would go and check
```

---

## Two things worth saying before you start

**The team did nothing wrong.** Their record is honest and their tally at the
bottom of it is correct. Everything that went wrong happened between the record and
the report, and that distinction is the whole exercise.

**One of the problems is arithmetic** and it is checkable in thirty seconds.
