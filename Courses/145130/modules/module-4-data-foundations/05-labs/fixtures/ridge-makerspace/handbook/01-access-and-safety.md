# Ridge Creek Makerspace: Access and Safety

Invented sample document. The Ridge Creek Makerspace is a teaching fixture for this
course and is not a real place.

## Who may be in the space

The makerspace is open to any student with a current membership record in the kiosk
system. A membership record is created when a student completes the orientation walk
through with a staff member. Membership does not expire during the school year.

A guest may enter with a member, and the member signs the guest in at the kiosk. A guest
may watch. A guest may not operate equipment.

## Hours

The space is open during the school day whenever a staff member is present, and after
school on Tuesday and Thursday until 4:30. It is closed when no staff member is in the
room, even if the door is unlocked.

## Eye protection

Eye protection is required within the yellow floor line at all times, including when you
are watching someone else work. There are loaner glasses in the bin by the door. Glasses
that sit on top of your head are not eye protection.

## Training holds

Some equipment carries a training hold. You may not sign out an item with a training
hold until a staff member records your training in the kiosk. Training is recorded per
item type, not per item, so training on one filament printer covers both of them.

The equipment that carries a training hold is the two filament printers, the resin
printer, the vinyl cutter, the soldering station, the oscilloscope, and the robot
chassis kit.

## Reporting damage

Report damage the same day, at the kiosk, before you leave. A member who reports damage
keeps full borrowing privileges. A member who returns damaged equipment without
reporting it loses borrowing privileges for two weeks.

The point of that rule is to make reporting the cheaper choice. Hidden damage costs the
next person their whole work session.

## Fire and evacuation

The extinguisher is beside the door and is rated for electrical fires. If a resin or
filament printer catches fire, leave it, pull the door alarm, and evacuate. Nothing in
this room is worth more than the people in it.
