# Laptop Carts

Invented sample document for this course.

## What a cart unit is

A cart unit is one laptop, its charger, and its sleeve. The number on the sleeve is the
unit number, and it is the number the kiosk records. Units 4 and 9 live in the
makerspace. The rest of the cart belongs to the building and is not part of this
catalog.

## Loan length

Laptop carts are a two day loan. Two days is short on purpose, because there are two
units and a long loan means nobody else gets one.

## What is on them

The lab image, which includes the editor, the language runtimes, and the slicer profile
for the filament printers. No account is signed in. Sign in with your own school
account, and sign out before you return the unit.

## What you may not do

Do not install software that needs an administrator password. Do not store the only copy
of your work on the laptop, because the unit is reimaged whenever it comes back with a
problem. Push your work to your repository before you return it.

## Condition

Unit 9 has a hinge problem and is marked as poor condition in the catalog. It still
works. Open and close it slowly, and do not carry it by the screen.

## Returning

Return the laptop charged, in its sleeve, with the charger. A unit returned without its
charger is an incomplete return.
