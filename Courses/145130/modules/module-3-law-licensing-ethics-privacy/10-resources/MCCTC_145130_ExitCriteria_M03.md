## The three habits, and these are what actually leave with you

**1. When a document makes a claim, open the source.** Not a summary of the source. The source. In
this module that was a license file, a regulation's text, or a publisher's own site, and it settled
an argument in under a minute every time.

**2. When something is missing, write down that it is missing.** `N/A` is a claim that the question
does not apply. `NO LICENSE FOUND` is a finding. **They look similar on a page and they do opposite
things to the next person who reads it.**

**3. When you do not know, say so and say who does.** Escalation is a full-credit answer in every
rubric in this module, and the reason is that it is the correct professional output. A confident
wrong answer is the weak one.
