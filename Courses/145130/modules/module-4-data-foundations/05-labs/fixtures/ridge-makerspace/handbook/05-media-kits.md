# Media Kit Checkout

Invented sample document for this course.

## What is in a kit

The camera kit contains the camera body, one lens, two batteries, a charger, a memory
card, and a strap, in a padded case with a contents card. The microphone kit contains
the microphone, a shock mount, a windscreen, a cable, and two batteries. The light kit
contains the panel, a stand, a diffuser, and a power adapter.

## Check the contents card

Count the contents against the card at the kiosk before you leave and again before you
return. The staff member counts with you. That is a two minute step that settles every
argument about who lost the windscreen.

## Loan length

Media kits are a three day loan.

## Memory cards

The card in the kit is a lab card. Copy your footage off it the day you return the kit,
because the card is formatted before the next loan. The makerspace keeps no copy of your
footage and is not a backup.

## Batteries

Both batteries go out charged. Return both batteries and the charger. A kit returned
with one battery is an incomplete return and stays on your record until the battery
comes back.

## Damage that is common and how to avoid it

The windscreen is the most commonly lost part in the makerspace. It is small, grey, and
it falls off in a bag. Put it in the case pocket, not loose in the bag.

The camera strap is the second most commonly lost part, usually because somebody takes
it off to use a tripod and leaves it on a desk.
