# Standard Operating Procedure: Resin 3D Printer

Invented sample document for this course.

## Why this one has more rules

Liquid resin is a skin and eye irritant before it is cured. Uncured resin is also a
waste disposal problem. Everything below exists because of those two facts.

## Personal protection

Nitrile gloves, eye protection, and the ventilation cabinet running. No exceptions and
no shortcuts. If the cabinet fan is not running, the printer does not run.

Do not wear the gloves outside the resin area. Gloves that touch a door handle move
resin onto the door handle.

## Before you print

Shake the resin bottle for thirty seconds. Check the vat for cured fragments and strain
the resin if you find any. A cured fragment in the vat is what turns one failed print
into three.

Confirm your training is recorded in the kiosk. The resin printer carries a training
hold.

## Washing and curing

Wash the part in the alcohol tank for five minutes, then cure it under the UV lamp for
the time on the resin bottle. A part that feels dry is not necessarily cured. Uncured
resin stays irritating.

## Waste

Used alcohol, paper towels, and failed prints go in the labelled resin waste bin, not in
the classroom trash. Cure failed prints under the lamp before they go in the bin, which
turns a liquid waste problem into a solid one.

## Loan length

The resin printer is a five day loan, which is shorter than the other fabrication
equipment, because it shares the ventilation cabinet with the spray booth and the
cabinet is the bottleneck.
