# Chemistry notes, unit 2. Written by the Study Buddy team.
Ionic bond: a bond formed when one atom transfers an electron to another atom
Covalent bond: a bond formed when two atoms share a pair of electrons
Molarity: the number of moles of solute per liter of solution
Catalyst: a substance that speeds up a reaction without being consumed by it
Isotope: an atom of an element with a different number of neutrons
Mole: the amount of a substance containing as many particles as there are in the standard reference amount
