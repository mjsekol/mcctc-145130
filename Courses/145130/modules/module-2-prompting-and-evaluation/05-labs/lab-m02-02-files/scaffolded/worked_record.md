## Step 10 · The details

Not reached. Steps 7 to 9 found no work to check the year, volume, or page range
against. **If step 8 had found a real paper with this title, this is where a wrong
volume or year would have shown up, and that would be a Validity failure rather
than a hallucination.**

---

## Step 11 · Verdict

**Fabricated.**

**On what evidence:** the venue returns nothing in two services under an exact
phrase search, the title returns nothing in three searches including one with the
subtitle removed, and the author has no traceable connection to the subject. Four
independent searches, all negative, recorded above.

**What would change this verdict:** any one of the three searches returning a
match. A single library record for the journal would move this to unresolved
immediately, and I would then search that journal's own index for the title.

## What this record does that a bad one does not

A bad record says "I looked and could not find it."

This one says **where** I looked, **what exactly** I typed, and **what came back**,
so that somebody who disagrees with me can repeat every step and either confirm it
or show me where I went wrong. That is the whole difference, and it is worth about
half the marks on this lab.
