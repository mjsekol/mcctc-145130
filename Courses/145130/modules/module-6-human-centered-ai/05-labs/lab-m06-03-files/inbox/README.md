# Inbox fixtures

Eight short notes. **Every one of them is invented.** No name, room, or
situation in this folder belongs to a real person, and nothing here came from a
form, a roster, or a message anybody actually sent.

They are written the way people actually write when they are annoyed and in a
hurry: no punctuation in places, two problems in one note, and one note that is
somebody saying thank you and reporting nothing at all.

`note-08.txt` is the one worth reading twice. It contains words from three
different categories, which is what makes the keyword rule and the service
disagree.
